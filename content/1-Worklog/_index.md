# 12-week graduation internship worklog

This worklog records the AWS learning process and the development of a **document management and security system on AWS**, from infrastructure fundamentals through implementation, testing, and final reporting.

**Week 1 (20/04 – 26/04):** [AWS overview and account setup](1.1-week1/)

**Week 2 (27/04 – 03/05):** [Identity and access management with AWS IAM](1.2-week2/)

**Week 3 (04/05 – 10/05):** [Build network infrastructure and servers on AWS](1.3-week3/)

**Week 4 (11/05 – 17/05):** [Explore storage, database, and serverless services](1.4-week4/)

**Week 5 (18/05 – 24/05):** [Analyze requirements and design the system architecture](1.5-week5/)

**Week 6 (25/05 – 31/05):** [Build APIs and document management functions](1.6-week6/)

**Week 7 (01/06 – 07/06):** [Complete document storage and management functions](1.7-week7/)

**Week 8 (08/06 – 14/06):** [Integrate the interface and complete service permissions](1.8-week8/)

**Week 9 (15/06 – 21/06):** [Strengthen system security and monitoring](1.9-week9/)

**Week 10 (22/06 – 28/06):** [Detect and assess security risks](1.10-week10/)

**Week 11 (29/06 – 05/07):** [Test and refine the system](1.11-week11/)

**Week 12 (06/07 – 12/07):** [Review and conclude the project](1.12-week12/)
